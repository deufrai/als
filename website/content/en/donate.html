Support ALS

Astro Live Stacker (ALS) is a free, open-source project maintained on personal time. If ALS helps you, a small donation makes a big difference.

Donate via Ko-fi

How your donations are used

  • Hardware to reproduce & fix issues — e.g. purchase a Raspberry Pi 5 to investigate platform-specific bugs.
  • Hardware to build platform-specific releases — e.g. purchase a second-hand Intel-based Mac to ship and verify macOS Intel builds.
  • Coffee ☕ — fuel for development and documentation.

Thank you for helping ALS reach more astro-imagers with better builds, faster fixes, and clearer docs.
