It’s finally happening.  
After years of pixel-pushing, photon-wrangling, and late-night debugging under the stars, **Astro Live Stacker is soon
reaching version 1.0** — our first truly **feature-complete** release. 🥳

## 🌌 What’s new?

The highlight of this milestone: **flat calibration**.  
Yep, the long-awaited ability to correct your subs using master flats is here — and it completes the calibration suite
alongside master darks. With this, ALS officially checks all the boxes we’d dreamed of when the project began.

## 🔭 Why it matters

This is more than “just another release.”  
It’s a turning point: ALS now covers the full live-stacking workflow from capture to calibrated, stacked, and stretched
images — all in real time, all open-source, all cross-platform.

## 🧪 Want to try it early?

Curious stargazers can already get their hands on preview builds and put the new flat calibration through its paces:  
👉 [**Testing builds**](https://als-app.org/nightlies/)

Feedback, bug reports, or even “hey, it worked!” messages are all welcome. You can drop your thoughts in our usual
channels — every bit of feedback helps polish the final 1.0 release.

## ❤️ Thanks, everyone

This journey wouldn’t have been possible without the testers, contributors, and the community that kept believing that
live stacking could be both *powerful* and *fun*.

So, fire up your gear, grab a few flats, and help us make **ALS v1.0** shine.  
The sky’s the limit — literally. 🌠
